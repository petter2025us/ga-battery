# Orlando Market Analysis: OUC Shift & Save vs Georgia Power

## Critical Finding: Orlando Is Not Viable

**Status:** OUC's Shift & Save program (the only TOU option for Orlando) launches **January 2027**. No TOU available to customers today.

**Spread Comparison:**

| Utility          | Peak Rate | Off-Peak | Spread     | Window                        |
| ---------------- | --------- | -------- | ---------- | ----------------------------- |
| Georgia Power OA | 29.79¢    | 2.19¢    | **27.60¢** | 2–7pm (5h); year-round summer |
| OUC Shift & Save | 10.70¢    | 4.60¢    | **6.10¢**  | 2–8pm (6h); year-round        |

**Spread ratio:** 6.10¢ ÷ 27.60¢ = **22%** of Georgia Power's spread.

## Battery Earnings Impact

Georgia Power arbitrage ceiling: **$459/yr** (10.5 kWh usable × 84 summer peak weekdays × 27.6¢ differential ÷ 12 months, then annualized with shoulder seasons).

OUC arbitrage ceiling (proportional to spread):

- **$459 × 0.22 ≈ $101/yr**

Compare to lease cost floor:

- **$106–194/mo** (at 8% capital, 30% §48E credit, $4k CAC, $10/mo O&M)

**Result: $101/yr earned vs $106–194/mo required = deal is dead by 1260% to 2300%.**

Georgia case was already failing by ~75%. Orlando case is worse by an order of magnitude.

## Timing Blocker

Even if the spread were adequate, **no customer in Orlando can use Shift & Save today**. The program launches January 2027. Any campaign starting now would:

1. Acquire leads for a product that doesn't exist
2. Promise savings that won't be available for 4+ months
3. Hope customers don't cancel leases when launch slips (standard for utility rollouts)

## What This Means

**Do not pursue arbitrage in Orlando.** The economics are structurally worse than Georgia, and the program doesn't exist yet.

**Backup power remains the only honest product.** Central Florida has real outage exposure (Hurricanes Ian 2022, Helene 2024, Milton 2024 produced multi-day outages). SAIDI data from the Florida PSC would determine if resilience is sellable at $106+/mo. That's a completely different business model:

- No complex tariff arbitrage
- No "personalized savings calculator"
- No low-friction funnel based on bill + phone
- Honest message: "Protect against grid failures"
- Price anchor: cost of a generator + fuel + maintenance
- Real compliance problem: home-solicitation cooling-off, FTSA, FDACS licensing

That pivot is defensible. The arbitrage pivot is not.
