## A/B TEST STRATEGY

### Days 1-3 (Daily Budget: $100-150)

- Creative A: $50 (fear angle)
- Creative B: $50 (ROI angle)
- Creative C: $50 (social proof angle)

### Day 3 Check-in (Evening)

- CPL (cost per lead)
- CTR (click-through rate)
- Form completion rate

**Pause:** Lowest performer  
**Double down:** Top 2 performers

### Days 4-5

- Winner A: $125/day
- Winner B: $125/day

### KPIs to Track (in Ads Manager)

| Metric                    | Target         | Go/No-Go                 |
| ------------------------- | -------------- | ------------------------ |
| **CPL**                   | <$30           | Pause at >$50            |
| **CTR**                   | >2%            | Pause at <1%             |
| **Form completion**       | >10% of clicks | Keep running             |
| **Total leads from $500** | 15-25          | 8+ = marginal, <8 = kill |

---

## EXPECTED OUTCOMES

From $500 spend over 5 days:

- ~15-20 clicks per creative (45-60 total clicks)
- ~5-8 form submissions (10-15% completion rate expected)
- **CPL: $50-100 (acceptable range for high-intent residential)**
- Of 8-15 leads, expect to convert 3-5 on phone calls to lease agreements

### Success Threshold

**15+ leads → Proceed to Week 2 (phone calls, lease confirmations)**  
**8-15 leads → Marginal; try $49/mo message or wait for full $1k spend**  
**<8 leads → Pivot; test different ZIP or messaging**

## TARGETING NOTES

- **ZIP codes:** 91942 (Ramona, priority), 92026 (El Cajon), 92084 (Ramona/Poway), 92021 (Descanso)
- **Frequency cap:** 2 per day per user (don't overwhelm)
- **Schedule:** Run continuously during ad window (tests run full 5 days, no day-of-week optimization yet)
- **Placement:** Feed + Stories (Instagram Stories often has lower CPL for this demographic)
