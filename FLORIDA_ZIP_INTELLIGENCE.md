## 1. Reliability — the claim has to survive scrutiny

The instinct "Florida = unreliable power = easy resilience sell" does not hold up territory-by-territory. This matters because an ad claiming outage frequency in a territory that just posted record-low outages is a substantiation problem, not a copywriting choice — same lesson as the Georgia bill-savings claim ([ga-battery-economics-do-not-close](ARCHITECTURE.md)).

| Utility             | 2025 reliability signal                                                              | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Read for resilience pitch                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Duke Energy Florida | ~64 min avg outage duration/customer, **excluding named storms** — best in 20+ years | [Duke Energy news release](https://news.duke-energy.com/releases/duke-energy-florida-highlights-record-low-outage-time-for-customers-in-2025)                                                                                                                                        | Blue-sky-day reliability is strong. Pitch must lean on **named-storm exclusion** — normal-day outages are rare, storm-day outages are the entire remaining risk. Backup-power-for-hurricanes, not backup-power-for-everyday-outages. |
| FPL                 | Lowest-ever outage **frequency** in 2025 (SAIFI), matched best-ever reliability      | [FPL newsroom](https://newsroom.fpl.com/Annual-Distribution-Reliability-Report-FPL-customers-experienced-lowest-ever-frequency-of-outages-in-2025), [Daily Energy Insider](https://dailyenergyinsider.com/news/51723-florida-power-light-matches-its-best-ever-reliability-in-2025/) | Same problem as Duke — frequency is down, so "constant outages" is not a defensible hook. Same storm-exclusion logic applies.                                                                                                        |
| Both                | County/service-area-level SAIDI/SAIFI breakdown                                      | `DATA GAP` — the granular numbers sit inside the [FPL 2025 filing](https://www.floridapsc.com/library/FILINGS/2025/06743-2025/06743-2025.pdf) and Duke's equivalent FL PSC filing, not in press releases                                                                             | **Action:** pull both PSC dockets directly (FPL filing above found; Duke's FL distribution reliability report needs a direct floridapsc.com docket search) before writing any county-specific reliability claim.                     |

**Conclusion:** the honest resilience pitch in both territories is **storm-specific**, not everyday-outage-specific. This actually _simplifies_ messaging — "backup power for the next named storm" is a narrower, more substantiable, and arguably higher-WTP claim than "your power is unreliable."

## 4. Utility storage/VPP programs — direct product-market fit signal

| Program                                  | Territory                                                                                    | Terms                                                                                                                  | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tesla VPP × SECO Energy                  | SECO Energy territory (Orlando-area co-op, parts of Lake/Marion/Orange/Sumter/Polk counties) | $1/kW-installed/mo enrollment + $0.30/kWh delivered during events, paid as SECO bill credits, up to ~$275/Powerwall/yr | [Tesla SECO VPP page](https://tesla.com/support/energy/virtual-power-plant/seco)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Duke Energy Florida Hunter's Creek pilot | Orlando (Orange County), Hunter's Creek neighborhood                                         | 75+ homes, Generac BESS, utility-owned/installed at **no cost to homeowner**, 10-year demand-response study            | [Duke news release](https://news.duke-energy.com/releases/duke-energy-florida-launches-residential-battery-storage-pilot-in-orlandos-hunters-creek-neighborhood), [Microgrid Knowledge](https://www.microgridknowledge.com/energy-storage/news/55357438/duke-energy-florida-installing-residential-battery-storage-in-10-year-grid-optimization-project) |

**This is the strongest single signal in this pass.** Two different utilities are actively paying for or subsidizing residential batteries in the Orlando metro / SECO footprint right now. That means:

- A live path to **utility/VPP revenue** exists in this specific geography — the one variable the Georgia model flagged as "the thing that could have flipped the sign, and it is not available" ([README.md](README.md)) may actually be available in SECO territory. Needs direct confirmation of whether SECO's VPP is open to _new_ Powerwall installs (i.e., a lease customer's system) or only existing owners.
- Duke's pilot is utility-funded and closed enrollment (75 homes, already running) — it's a signal of utility appetite, not a channel we can sell into directly. But it validates that Duke FL sees resilience/BESS value in exactly this metro.

**Action:** confirm SECO VPP eligibility rules for a newly-leased (third-party-owned) Powerwall — the program page listed owner eligibility criteria but we haven't confirmed whether a TPO/lease structure qualifies for "residential electric account in good standing + interconnection application," which sounded owner-agnostic but needs a direct call to SECO or Tesla energy support to confirm.
