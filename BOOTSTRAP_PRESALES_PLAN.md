# Bootstrap Pre-Sales Model

## Zero-Capital WTP Validation + Pilot Funding

**Strategy:** Collect customer pre-orders to fund the pilot itself. No survey cost. Customers validate the idea with their money.

---

## The Model

Instead of:

```
Pay $8-15k for WTP study → Wait 5 weeks → Decide to pilot → Run pilot
```

Do this:

```
Landing page (free/cheap) → Micro ads ($500-1k test) → Pre-orders →
Use deposits to fund batteries → Install + close → Repeat
```

**Cash flow:** Customers → Deposits → Battery procurement → Installations → Revenue

---

## Phase 1: Landing Page + Micro Ad Test (Week 1-2)

### Landing Page (Free/Cheap)

**Build:** Simple one-pager (Carrd, Wix free, or Webflow $14/mo)

- Hero: "PSPS Protection: $60/mo for 48-Hour Backup Power"
- Problem: Last 3 PSPS events in your area (dates, duration)
- Solution: Battery backup (visual)
- Proof: SAIDI/PSPS data + testimonials (use neighbors as beta testers)
- CTA: "Reserve Your System - $60/mo, 90-Day Installation Window"
- Form: Name, phone, address, email
- Privacy: "We'll call within 24 hours to confirm"

**Cost:** $0-50 for domain (or use free Carrd subdomain)

### Micro Ad Test ($500-1k spend)

**Platform:** Facebook + Instagram (lowest CAC for local geo-targeting)

**Targeting:**

- ZIP codes: 91942, 92026, 92084, 92021 (San Diego PSPS zone)
- Age: 35-65
- Income: Household >$75k (proxy for homeowners)
- Interests: "Power outage," "Emergency preparedness," "Solar panels," "Home security"

**Ad creative (3 variations):**

1. **Fear-based:** "Last PSPS cut power for 72 hours. 1,200 families in El Cajon lost $400+ to spoiled food. Battery backup: $60/mo."
2. **Opportunity:** "Stop paying $1,000+ per outage. Battery backup starts at $60/mo."
3. **Community:** "500+ families in Ramona already signed up. 48-hour backup power, $60/mo, zero upfront."

**Budget:** $500-1k over 1-2 weeks

- A/B test all 3 creatives
- Daily budget: $50-100
- Pause underperformers after 2-3 days

**Success metrics:**

- CPL target: <$30 (landing page + pre-order form)
- Form submission rate: >3% of clicks
- From $500 spend: 15-25 pre-order leads expected

### Offer Structure

**Pre-order terms:**

- Price: $60/mo, 25-year lease (matches model)
- Installation: 90-120 days
- Commitment: Refundable deposit ($200-500) to confirm commitment
- Coverage: "48-hour PSPS protection, outage insurance positioning"

**Deposit mechanics:**

- Non-refundable if customer cancels
- Applied to first lease payment if customer proceeds
- Holds seat in 90-day installation window

## Phase 2: Pre-Order Validation (Week 2-4)

### Success Thresholds

| Outcome                             | Meaning                         | Next Step                         |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| **>15 pre-orders from $500 spend**  | WTP validated at $60/mo         | → Scale to Phase 3                |
| **8-15 pre-orders from $500 spend** | Marginal; test $49/mo messaging | → Re-run ads at lower price point |
| **<8 pre-orders from $500 spend**   | Insurance angle fails           | → Archive, pivot to alternatives  |

### If Validated: Scale Ad Spend

- **If 15+ pre-orders:** Increase budget to $2k-3k over next 2 weeks
- **Target:** 50+ pre-order deposits ($200-500 each) = $10k-25k capital raised
- **Use capital for:** Battery procurement + installation labor (pilot phase)

### Sales Call Process (Overnight Response)

Call each pre-order lead within 24 hours:

**Script:**

> "Hi [Name], thanks for showing interest in PSPS protection. I want to make sure you understand what we're offering:
>
> - $60 per month for 25 years
> - 13.5 kWh battery, installed in your garage
> - Full backup power for 48 hours during PSPS or other outages
> - All maintenance and insurance included
> - Commitment: We install within 90-120 days
>
> To hold your spot, we need a $300 deposit, which applies to your first payment if you proceed.
>
> Are you ready to move forward?"

**Outcomes:**

- Yes → Collect deposit (Stripe, PayPal)
- Maybe → Schedule follow-up call in 3 days
- No → Ask why; capture objection for next ad iteration

### Deposit Collection & Accounting

**Platform:** Stripe or PayPal (both allow instant deposits to bank)

**Tracking:**

- Track which ZIP → which ad creative → conversion rate
- Track deposit rate (% of leads that pay)
- Build drop-off funnel: clicks → form → call → deposit

## Phase 3: Pilot Installation (Month 2-4)

### Capital Raised

Assume 50 pre-order deposits @ $300 avg = **$15,000**

Breakdown of use:

- Battery cost: $7-8k per unit (wholesale/installer pricing, lower than consumer)
- Installation labor: $2-3k per unit
- Permits/inspections: $500-1k per unit
- Margin for mistakes/cancellations: $2-3k buffer

**First 10-15 installations** can be funded from deposits.

### Installation Partners

Partner with local electricians/installers in target ZIP codes:

- Find via Google Maps ("electrician near Ramona CA")
- Pitch: "We bring you customers; you install. 25% commission on lease."
- Example: Lease $60/mo × 12 mo × 25% = $180/month recurring per customer

**Benefit:** They fund the labor, you fund the battery. Lower capital requirement for you.

### Pilot Metrics

For first 15 customers:

| Metric                      | Target                    | Why It Matters              |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Installation completion** | 90%+ on time              | Credibility for next cohort |
| **Customer satisfaction**   | NPS >60                   | Retention + word-of-mouth   |
| **Outage activation**       | 1+ PSPS event in 120 days | Prove insurance value       |
| **Renewal rate**            | 80%+ continue past year 1 | Unit economics work         |

## Phase 4: Repeat & Scale (Month 4+)

**If pilot successful:**

1. Customers provide testimonials ("We were protected during the Dec PSPS")
2. Use testimonials in next cohort ads
3. Increase ad spend to $3-5k/month
4. Target additional ZIP codes (expand to LA County, other CA utilities)
5. Collect pre-orders → Fund installations → Repeat

**Cash flow loop:**

- Month 1: 50 pre-orders, $15k deposits
- Month 2-4: Install first 15 customers from pre-orders + first deposits
- Month 4: Launch second cohort ads, collect 50 new pre-orders
- Deposits from cohort 2 fund installation of cohort 1 backlog
- Month 6+: Revenue-generating (lease payments > new customer acquisition cost)

## Financial Modeling: Break-Even

### Assumptions

- Lease price: $60/mo = $720/year
- CAC: $30 per lead (micro ad spend)
- Conversion: 3% (1 per ~33 leads)
- CAC per closed customer: $30/0.03 = $1,000
- Payback period: $1,000 ÷ $720 = 1.4 years
- LTV (assuming 5-year avg tenure): $720 × 5 = $3,600

### Unit Economics

- LTV:CAC ratio = 3,600 : 1,000 = **3.6x** (healthy; >3x is good)
- Annual revenue per customer: $720
- Payback: 17 months

### Initial Capital Requirement

- **Phase 1-2:** $500-1k ad spend (test)
- **Phase 3:** $15k deposits (from pre-orders, not out-of-pocket)
- **First 15 installations:** $15k of pre-order deposits covers batteries + labor + buffer

**Total out-of-pocket needed:** ~$1-2k for landing page + initial ad test.

## Risk Mitigation

### Risk: Deposits Come In But No PSPS Event

**Mitigation:**

- Include maintenance visits (annual inspection) so customer sees value
- Offer "test activation" demo (show how system works without real outage)
- Provide energy arbitrage tips (use battery for TOU shifting) to generate secondary value

### Risk: Installation Delays (Permit Issues, Labor Shortage)

**Mitigation:**

- Clear installation window in pre-order: "90-120 days, no guarantees"
- Partner with multiple installers to spread risk
- If delay >120 days, offer refund or lease price reduction ($55/mo) for wait time

### Risk: Customer Cancels After Deposit

**Mitigation:**

- Non-refundable deposits reduces churn incentive
- But: Allow ONE penalty-free cancellation in first 30 days (goodwill, reduces buyer's remorse)
- Collect phone/email upfront; nurture leads during 90-day wait

### Risk: Outage Never Happens

**Mitigation:**

- PSPS is 4-5x per year in high-risk zones; odds are good
- In case of multi-year no-outage, offer exit clause after year 3 at $0 penalty
- But: Include maintenance value + TOU arbitrage messaging so customer sees ongoing value

**Week 3-4:**

- Collect pre-order deposits

**Decision point (Week 4):**

- **Go:** 15+ pre-orders, $5k+ deposits → Partner with installers, begin Phase 3
- **No-Go:** <15 pre-orders → Archive insurance angle, pursue alternatives

## Alternative Paths (If Even $1k Test Budget Is Tight)

### Organic Pre-Sales (Zero Ad Spend)

1. Post in Nextdoor for each target ZIP code
2. LinkedIn posts about PSPS resilience (if you have network)
3. Local SDG&E community groups (Facebook, Nextdoor)
4. Nextdoor ads (cheaper than Meta, highly geo-targeted; $300-500 budget)

**Timeline:** 2-4 weeks to collect 10 pre-orders
**CAC:** $0 (organic reach)
**Tradeoff:** Slower, but free

### Contractor-First Approach

1. Partner with local electricians/HVAC installers in high-PSPS ZIPs
2. Give them 3-5 pre-built "PSPS protection" leads to pitch to their existing customers
3. They handle sales; you provide pre-order link + deposit mechanism
4. They split commissions 50/50 with you (or 70/30 based on who closes)

**Timeline:** 1-2 weeks to recruit partners, 4 weeks to 10 pre-orders
**CAC:** $0-200 (affiliate commissions only, not upfront)
**Advantage:** Leverages existing relationships, lower risk
