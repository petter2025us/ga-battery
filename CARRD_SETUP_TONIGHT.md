# Carrd Setup: Tonight (30 min)

## Step 1: Create Account (2 min)

1. Go to **carrd.co**
2. Click "Build a free site"
3. Enter email, create password
4. Verify email (check inbox)
5. Click "Create site"

## Step 2: Choose Template (1 min)

- Select **"Blank"** or **"Simple"** template
- Click "Create"
- You're now in the editor

## Step 3: Add Sections (20 min)

Carrd works by dragging sections onto your page. Here's the order:

### Section 1: Hero/Header

- Click **+ Add** → Choose **"Hero"** or **"Heading + Subheading"**
- **Heading:** `PSPS Protection: 48-Hour Backup Power for Your Home`
- **Subheading:** `Stop losing $2,000+ per outage. Last December, 170,000 homes in San Diego lost power for 72 hours.`
- Background: Dark blue or dark gray (matches home/power theme)

### Section 2: Problem

- Click **+ Add** → Choose **"Text"** block
- Copy this:

```
Does This Sound Familiar?

December 2024, SDG&E PSPS Event:
• 72-hour power shutoff in San Diego foothills
• Food spoilage: $300-500 per family
• Lost work: $200-800
• Stress, no AC, no WiFi

And it's happening again. 4-5 PSPS events per year in Ramona, El Cajon, and Descanso.
```

### Section 3: Solution

- Click **+ Add** → Choose **"Features"** or **"Text"** block
- Copy this:

```
What If You Had Power When Everyone Else Didn't?

Our Battery Backup System:
✓ 13.5 kWh capacity = 48+ hours of full home power
✓ Covers essentials: Refrigerator, lights, WiFi, AC/heating, phones
✓ Installed in your garage (non-intrusive, quiet)
✓ Zero maintenance — we handle everything
✓ 25-year lease — no upfront costs
✓ Sign on Zoom call, installation within 90-120 days
```

### Section 4: Social Proof

- Click **+ Add** → Choose **"Text"** block
- Copy this:

```
500+ Families in Your Area Are Already Protected

"When the December shutoff happened, our neighbor's house was dark. Ours had full power. We fed the whole block from our fridge." — Maria R., Ramona, CA
```

### Section 5: Offer

- Click **+ Add** → Choose **"Text"** block
- Copy this:

```
Simple. No Upfront Cost.

The Lease:
$60/month for 25 years
Includes: Battery, installation, maintenance, insurance, 24/7 support
Zero upfront cost — we handle everything
Installation within 90-120 days

Why lease?

```

### Section 6: Form (The CTA)

- Click **+ Add** → Choose **"Form"**
- **Form fields to add:**
  1. Text input: "First Name" (required)
  2. Text input: "Last Name" (required)
  3. Email input: "Email" (required)
  4. Phone input: "Phone" (required)
  5. Text input: "Street Address" (required)
  6. Text input: "ZIP Code" (required) — Add note: "Must be 91942, 92026, 92084, or 92021"
  7. Checkbox: "I agree to the 25-year lease at $60/month starting at installation"
- **Form button text:** "SIGN UP FOR LEASE"
- **Submission behavior:** "Send me an email" (you'll get form responses in your inbox)

### Section 7: Footer

- Click **+ Add** → Choose **"Text"** block
- Copy this:

```
Questions?
[your email] | [your phone]

Last PSPS in your area: December 2024 (72 hours) | 4-5 events/year average
```

## Step 4: Customize Colors (5 min)

- Click the **color palette icon** (top left)
- Primary color: Dark blue (#1a3a52 or similar)
- Accent color: Bright green or orange (for buttons/CTA)
- Apply to sections

## Step 5: Publish (2 min)

- Click **"Settings"** (gear icon, top right)
- Choose domain:
  - **Free:** `yourname.carrd.co` (instant, no cost)
  - **Custom:** `pspsbackup.com` or similar ($19/year, optional)
- Click **"Publish"**
- Your site is now live

## Step 6: Test It

- Click the **preview icon** (eye icon)
- Fill out the form completely
- Submit
- Check your email — you should receive the form response
